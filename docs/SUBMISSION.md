Final pitch:
Higher Ground is a bilingual (English and Turkish) 2.5D atmospheric platformer built in Godot 4 with original Blender 3D assets, where a lone penguin climbs from melting sea ice through a litter strewn mountain slope to a wind swept summit research station, turning climate anxiety into tangible actions and hope ahead of COP31 in Antalya.

Features:
1. Three distinct chapters with climate mechanics: melting ice floes and solar heat exposure in Chapter 1 (Shore), source waste sorting across Paper, Plastic, and Glass bins to clear barriers in Chapter 2 (Slope), and wind gusts with a solar lamp connection in Chapter 3 (Summit).
2. Responsive 2.5D platformer controller with coyote time, jump buffering, and Shift belly slide mechanic with procedural waddle and slide animations.
3. Dynamic Hope system (0 to 100) driven by player choices (helping a stranded chick, sorting recyclable waste, planting a pine seed, connecting a solar lamp) that shifts the world color grading and unlocks three distinct endings (Walked Away, Looked Back, Went Back Down).
4. Six verified climate and Zero Waste fact cards backed by IPCC AR6, Nature Communications, One Earth, UN GA Resolution 77/161, and IEA Renewables reports, plus dual mode narrative monologues (bottom cinematic subtitles and floating callouts in front of the penguin).
5. Full instant bilingual localization (English and Turkish) and custom CC0 synthesized audio (wind ambience, calm music, footsteps, jump, land, ice crack, pickup, sort feedback, and UI clicks) with in game pause and settings controls.

Focus areas:
1. Cryosphere loss and species range shift toward higher ground (IPCC AR6, British Antarctic Survey).
2. Zero Waste and source separation of paper, plastic, and glass waste (UN International Day of Zero Waste, 30 March).
3. Renewable solar and wind energy transition and COP31 awareness (IEA, UNFCCC COP31 Antalya).
